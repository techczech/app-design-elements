# Third-party acknowledgements

App Design Elements is maintained by Dominik Lukeš. The external works below informed identified portions of its guidance. Their authorship remains with their respective authors. Acknowledging a source does not adopt its entire workflow, aesthetic preferences, architecture or licence for unrelated original content.

The specific requirement to show a purpose for every command, distinguish core from supplementary commands, and prioritise Start reading aloud for broad task intent came from Dominik's own request. It is not represented as an imported ranking algorithm. Likewise, the two-top-bar rule and supplied overlap examples originate in his requirements and observations.

## Platform documentation and other reading

[Electron title-bar documentation](https://www.electronjs.org/docs/latest/tutorial/custom-title-bar), [Electron keyboard-shortcut documentation](https://www.electronjs.org/docs/latest/tutorial/keyboard-shortcuts) and [Microsoft title-bar documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/windows/apps/develop/title-bar) ground the platform criteria. APIs and availability still need checking against the app's actual framework version.

Additional research-only sources include [Vercel's review adapter](https://github.com/vercel-labs/agent-skills), [Native-Feel Cross-Platform Desktop](https://github.com/yetone/native-feel-skill), [wshobson's UI-design collection](https://github.com/wshobson/agents/tree/main/plugins/ui-design), [SwiftUI Design Skill](https://github.com/Wholiver/swiftui-design-skill), [SwiftUI Pro](https://github.com/twostraws/SwiftUI-Agent-Skill), and [Bart Reardon's Mac behaviour skill](https://github.com/bartreardon/skills/tree/main/mac-arsed-mac-app). These are credited as reading, not dependencies. No text from Vercel's small review adapter was copied; no root licence was found in that adapter repository at the inspected revision. Its linked guideline source has its own retained MIT notice.
